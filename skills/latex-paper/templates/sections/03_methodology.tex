\subsection{Problem Formulation}
Let $\vecx \in \R^{d}$ represent the input representation. We seek a mapping $f_{\vecw}: \R^d \to \R^k$ parameterized by weights $\vecw$:
\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:objective}
    \vecw^* = \argmin_{\vecw} \E_{(\vecx, \vecy)} \left[ \mathcal{L}(f_{\vecw}(\vecx), \vecy) \right].
\end{equation}
